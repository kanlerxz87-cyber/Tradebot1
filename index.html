Official sources used across the site

  • API environments — demo and production REST/WebSocket base URLs, request signing.
  • Orderbook responses — bids only; asks are implied as $1.00 − the opposite side's bid.
  • Fee schedule — 0.07 taker / 0.0175 maker, no settlement or membership fee (effective 2026-07-07).
  • Market lifecycle and settlement — how a market is finalized yes or no.
  • Demo environment — mock funds, separate credentials, and Kalshi's warning that demo prices may not reflect real markets.
