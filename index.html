Kalshi · demo environment · research, not advice

Five topics. One desk. Every number traced to a source.

Gas prices, elections, culture, crypto and golf — scanned continuously for moves of $0.10 or more, charted with price and resting liquidity, tracked to a YES/NO resolution, and traded by four rule-based strategies in a simulated book. Next to every alert: the links Kalshi publishes for the contract, and a date-restricted news search you can open to judge the move yourself.

Primary · autonomous

Research desk →

Scheduled collector, two alert chats (all moves, and the model's high-ROI subset), price chart with 30-second-to-all windows, orderbook depth, resolution tracker, four strategies with per-type analysis, and a source link on every table row.

 Secondary · interactive

Market monitor →

Polls public Kalshi endpoints from your own browser session, groups open markets by event, ranks by estimated 24-hour traded dollars, and shows live orderbook depth for any contract.

 Prototype · simulated data

Prototype →

Kept for review. Its displayed prices come from a simulated fallback and a local strategy simulator, not from Kalshi. A credentialed Python client sits beside it and ships no keys.

Two limits worth reading first. No Kalshi demo credentials exist in this repository, so the Kalshi demo account itself is not connected — the simulated book runs on an internal ledger and the portfolio page says so instead of estimating account data. And no causality is ever asserted: alerts show Kalshi's own contract links plus a date-restricted search, clearly labelled as unverified. Both are tracked on Flagged for review.
Demo and paper trading only — no production order path exists in this repository. Kalshi market data belongs to Kalshi; see the official API documentation and the fee schedule. Source: github.com/kanlerxz87-cyber/Tradebot1 · repo review.
