Known limits, stated rather than hidden. The public site is refreshed by scheduled CI cycles (15 and 30 minutes), not by a WebSocket; a browser session can therefore see a swing that the published feed has not recorded yet, and 10¢ moves between two cycles can be missed. Prices come from Kalshi's public market data; execution is simulated in this repository. No demo-account credentials are configured, so no demo balance or fill is shown — the paper book is not a demo account. Every item that could not be verified is on the Flagged for review page.
What sits next to a swing. Each alert in the desk carries two separate blocks: Contract sources — Kalshi's series page, the market object, the filed terms and the exchange's own named settlement source, all published by Kalshi — and Possible catalysts, which are date-restricted news searches rather than asserted causes. The repository has no verifiable news feed, so it shows the time window and lets you judge instead of guessing at a headline. Details: repository review, defect D4.
