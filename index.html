Five topics. One desk. Every number traced to an official source.

Gas prices, elections, culture, crypto and golf — scanned continuously for moves of $0.10 or more, charted with price and resting liquidity, tracked to a YES/NO resolution, and traded by four rule-based strategies in a simulated book.

Autonomous

Research desk →

The collector-driven desk: 15-minute scheduled cycles, two alert chats (all moves and the model's high-ROI subset), price chart with 30s–all windows, orderbook depth, resolution tracker, four strategies with per-type analysis, and a source link on every table row.

 Interactive

Market monitor →

The browser-side monitor: polls public Kalshi endpoints from your own session, groups open markets by event, sorts by estimated 24h traded dollars, and shows live orderbook depth for any contract you pick.

 Board

Trade board →

A third implementation, in Node with no runtime dependencies: events ranked by resting dollars inside each of the five topics, two scrolling alert chats for moves of $0.10 or more, a price chart with 60s–24h windows and an order-book ladder, five simulated strategies, and a source link on every table row.

Prototype →

The first standalone prototype, kept for review: simulated fallback data, a local strategy simulator and a credentialed Python client. Clearly separated from verified market data.
