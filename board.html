Kalshi demo market board

A live board over Kalshi's demo exchange, restricted to five topics: gas prices, elections, culture, crypto and golf. It ranks markets by the dollar notional resting on them, watches for price swings of $0.10 or more in either direction, and follows each market until the exchange finalizes a yes or no outcome.

Read this before trusting a number

Everything below is true of this build and is published on the review page too.

  • Demo markets use mock funds; Kalshi warns demo prices may not reflect real markets.
  • Alerts compare two polls. A move between polls can be missed (F-11).
  • The filtered feed is a project score out of 100, not a return forecast (F-10).
  • No article is ever claimed to have caused a move (F-12).
