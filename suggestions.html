Interface ideas that cost almost nothing

Keyboard-first alert triage

J/K to move down the feed, Enter to open the market, S to star it. Traders keep hands on the keyboard; a dozen alerts during a move is where a mouse costs you.

Pin a market to the top bar

A sticky strip with the last price of three pinned markets, visible on every page. Cheap to build, and it turns five separate pages into one workspace.

Show the alert's own context inline

For each alert: the spread at the time, the contracts resting, and the fee for one contract at that price. That is the difference between "it moved" and "you could have traded it".

Make empty states actionable

When a topic has no open market, offer the nearest series that does, with a one-click jump. An empty page should suggest where to look next.

Sound off by default, one alert sound available

An audible cue for a qualifying swing, muted unless the reader turns it on. Never autoplay: a dashboard that beeps uninvited gets closed.

Offline-first, with a visible read-only mode

Cache the last successful API read and label it clearly when the network drops, instead of showing an error where the data should be.

What I would not add. A green "buy" badge, a confidence percentage, or a leaderboard of "best trades". Each of those turns an observation into advice, and this dashboard does not have the evidence to give advice. The brief's phrase "high ROI" is the exact pressure that produces those features; flag F-10 explains why this build refuses it and what it does instead.
