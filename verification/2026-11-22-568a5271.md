## 3. Defects found and fixed

### D1 — Every published board page carried a relative age that was wrong on arrival, and the build gate was really a clock

`scripts/build-site.mjs` baked `${stamp(newest)} (${relative(newest)})` into the footer of all 19
pages, and `--check` compared the committed page against a fresh render while normalising only the
`data-generated` attribute. Two consequences:

* the served page said "Newest observation: 2026-11-22 07:00Z (**24m ago**)" and kept saying it
  forever, so a static page stated a fact that became false within the hour;
* `node scripts/build-site.mjs --check` returned **exit 1 with all 19 pages listed as stale** whenever
  the build was more than ~90 seconds old. CI passed only because CI rebuilds and checks in the same
  minute; a human running the documented command got a failure list that looked like a broken site.

Fixed by rendering the age in the browser from an absolute `<time datetime data-obs-iso>` stamp, with
the relative slot empty in the HTML. `--check` is now deterministic — confirmed by rebuilding, waiting,
and re-checking (`Site is up to date (19 pages)`). Regression tests:
`tests/site-determinism.test.mjs` (3 tests: no baked `\d+ ago` in any generated page, footer shape,
`--check` passes).

### D2 — A published row could contradict itself, and the second sample could not raise an alert

`collector.py` takes a second sample inside a cycle ("finer chart resolution") and wrote back **only**
`price`. `mid`, `yes_bid`, `yes_ask` and the derived `notional_24h_est` stayed on the first sample.
Observed in the committed 07:21Z snapshot: `KXSOL15M-26NOV220230-30` quoted `price=0.695` beside
`mid=0.53` (14¢ apart) and `KXBNB15M` 0.615 vs 0.555. The swing detector reads `mid`, so a move the
second sample had just measured could not raise an alert — the exact failure the brief cares about.

Fixed by replacing the whole row with the newer response (and recomputing the ticker index), plus a
regression test that drives a two-sample cycle with a 14¢ move and asserts every price field and the
notional come from the same response. `cli verify` now also checks, on every run: price range,
`bid <= ask`, price/mid agreement, notional arithmetic, alert threshold, alert direction, alert source
link. Warnings are printed but not fatal, so a stale snapshot cannot block the collector.

### D3 — Documentation that overstated the tree

* `README.md` said **48 tests** (145 exist), **32 assertions** (39 exist), and that alerts are "stored
  with … candidate news links" when no alert carried any such field. Corrected, and the field now
  genuinely exists (see §4).
* The trade board's `data/live/feed.json` kept a labelled "worked example" row at the top of the live
  feed indefinitely. The Node collector now drops the example as soon as one genuine swing exists
  (`tools/collector.mjs`), so a live chat cannot be padded with a row that is not a live alert.

### D4 — The trade board's "what is in the feeds right now" notice was hardcoded

`templates/dashboard.tpl` stated "The committed record has no observation cycles in it" as fixed
prose. After the 07:29Z collector run the record held one cycle, 60 tracked markets and 60
observations, and the sentence was simply false. It is now generated from `data/live/feed.json`
(`{{FEED_STATE}}`, three cases: swings present, cycles run but quiet, no cycles yet). The rendered page
now reads: "The collector has run 1 time (latest 2026-11-22 07:29Z) and is tracking 60 markets, but no
market has yet been observed at two different times $0.10 or more apart."

### D5 — 25 series reads were refused with HTTP 429 and the shortfall was invisible

The 07:29Z Node cycle logged `too_many_requests` for 12 crypto, 12 golf and 1 culture series, so those
series contributed no markets and the topic totals silently under-reported coverage. `tools/kalshi.mjs`
had no pacing and no retry at all. It now spaces requests (300 ms default), honours `Retry-After`,
retries 429/503 with exponential backoff up to three times, and reports its pacing statistics
(`stats()`), which each collector run records under `runs[].requests`. Four new tests in
`tests/kalshi.test.mjs` cover retry-after, retry exhaustion, a non-retryable 404, and the pacing pause.
Published as flag **F-18** on the board's flagged page. The per-series failures were already kept in
`data/live/state.json` under `coverage.*.errors`; nothing was suppressed to hide them.

### Feature gap closed: "what may have caused this jump"

The brief asks for links to sites that may have caused a ≥ $0.10 move. No Kalshi field reports a
cause, and this project has no news feed, so inventing one would be fabrication. `swings.py` now adds
two **search** links per alert, built from the market's own title (Google News, DuckDuckGo), plus the
two official record links, with `context_note` saying plainly that they are not evidence of a cause.
Rendered in both alert chats next to the ticker. Alerts recorded before this change render without
them. Published as flag **F16**.
