Tradebot1 is a live command center for the Kalshi demo exchange (mock funds). Your browser reads the exchange directly, so the prices on it are as current as the exchange is. It covers gas prices, elections, culture, crypto and golf — and nothing else — shows the biggest one-day movers, fires alerts on contract moves of $0.10 or more in either direction, keeps those alerts in two scrolling feeds (every move, and the subset that passes a published screen), charts price against time, and prices the order book in contracts and dollars.

1 · Biggest movers, now

Contracts whose exchange-published one-day change is ≥ $0.10, sorted by size, with the source object behind every row. The two alert chats sit beside the chart.

2 · Chart any contract

X is time (30 s to 1 day), Y is contract price ($0.00–$1.00). History is backfilled from the official candlestick endpoint; live reads are layered on top and the panel says which is which.

3 · Follow it to YES/NO

Every followed event stays on the resolution tracker until the exchange publishes a result. A closed market with no result is shown as unresolved, never guessed.

4 · Try a strategy

Paper trades are simulated in this browser with the published taker fee. The demo-account panel reads your own Kalshi demo account if you connect it, and explains the browser-signing limitation first.

The five verticals

⛽ Gas Prices

Retail gasoline and diesel contracts (series KXAAAGAS*), settled on a published energy-agency figure. Identified by the official series and the oil/energy tags.

How the command center works

1 · Connect

The dashboard calls the Kalshi demo REST API directly from your browser (public market data needs no key). If the browser can't reach the API — or you're offline — it offers a clearly-labeled simulated feed instead. Your demo API keys, if you add them in Portfolio → Settings, never leave your browser except to Kalshi's demo hosts.

2 · Track & alert

Open contracts across the five verticals are polled continuously. Any contract moving ≥ $0.10 (vs its session reference or a 2-minute window) posts to the All Events feed; if the Move also scores ≥ 65 on the transparent high-ROI model, it posts to the Model-Approved feed. Click any message to load the contract.

3 · Decide & simulate

Interactive price chart (seconds → hours), full order-book ladder with contract counts at each price, resolution tracking (YES/NO) on the Events page, five backtested strategy archetypes in the Strategy Lab, and a paper portfolio you can reconcile against your real demo account.
