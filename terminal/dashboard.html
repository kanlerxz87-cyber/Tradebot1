Legend & how to read

GAS ELECTIONS CULTURE CRYPTO GOLF

Topic is assigned from the exchange's own series ticker, then its tags, then its category, and only then from words in the title. Each row shows which of those matched, so you can check it. Golf, for example, is a tag under Sports — not a category — so a golf event titled “Presidents Cup” is no longer filed under Elections.

Model score = 30 base + liquidity rank (≤ +25) + tight spread (≤ +20) + decisive move ≥ $0.10 (+15) / ≥ $0.05 (+8) + entry in $0.55–$0.85 (+8) + open interest ≥ 50k (+7).

Alert trigger = |price − reference| ≥ $0.10, where the reference resets after each alert and after any gap longer than 10 minutes. A quote that stops updating never alerts. 90-second per-contract cooldown.

Possible drivers link to news search and official reference sources for the vertical. They are candidates to check, not proven causes of any move.

SIM = simulated market (offline fallback). Resolutions (YES/NO) are tracked on Events & Resolution.
