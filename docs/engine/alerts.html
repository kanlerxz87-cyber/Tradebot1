Alert feeds

The rule is exact: a contract's observed price changed by $0.10 or more in either direction. The dashboard keeps two feeds of the same rule.

What price is watched. The mid of the best YES bid and the implied YES ask when the book is two-sided and uncrossed. On a one-sided or crossed book, the last traded price. When there was no trade behind the move, the alert is labelled quote-only so a stale quote is never presented as a real repricing.

High ROI (model-selected) 4

The same rule, additionally filtered by a trade-quality score computed from the size of the move, the contracts actually available within 10 cents, the spread and fee drag, the time left before the market closes, and whether the move was confirmed by a trade. The weights are published on the methodology page. This is a heuristic ranking of opportunity quality — not a probability of profit.
