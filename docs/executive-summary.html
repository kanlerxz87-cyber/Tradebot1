Executive summary

A focused research desk, with a clear line between evidence and execution.

Read the full brief ↗
THE OBJECTIVE

Five topics. Source-linked decisions.

Collect and organize official Kalshi demo observations for gas prices, elections, culture, crypto and golf. Follow ≥10¢ movements, inspect depth, compare hypothetical strategies and retain tracked contracts through final settlement.

EvidenceEvery market row links to its official source.
SeparationPublished snapshots ≠ live feeds ≠ paper accounts ≠ demo execution.
HonestyNo verified ROI, causal news, live account balance or exchange-wide ranking is claimed.

What is delivered

Software capability is distinct from verified deployment connectivity.

Public research dashboard

Five topic subpages, grouped events, source timestamps, search, CSV export, time-window charts, depth and dual scrolling feeds.

Demo-only service

REST collection, authenticated WebSocket adapter, sequence recovery, SQLite history, SSE and protected V2 order routes. Not connected to an account here.

Strategy laboratory

Independent momentum and mean-reversion paper ledgers, exposure caps, final YES/NO settlement and gross-only analysis.
