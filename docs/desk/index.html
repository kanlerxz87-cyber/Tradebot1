03Price movement and liquidity

X axis is time, Y axis is the contract price in dollars. Pick a window from 30 seconds to all history. The depth panel shows how many contracts rest at each price.

04Swing alerts — two live chats

Every alert is a recorded move of at least $0.10 in either direction, compared with the previous collection cycle. The right-hand chat only shows alerts that pass the model filter. Inside each message: Contract sources are links Kalshi itself publishes (series page, market object, filed terms, and the exchange's named settlement source). Possible catalysts are date-restricted news searches, clearly marked unverified — this repository cannot prove what moved a price, so it shows you the window instead of guessing.
