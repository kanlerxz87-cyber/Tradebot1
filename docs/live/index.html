Every ±10¢ move, two feeds, and the book behind it.

The console ranks open events by traded dollars, watches a fixed set of markets, and records a swing only when two of its own observations differ by $0.10 or more. The first feed shows every swing; the second ranks them with a published 0–100 triage score. Selecting a market draws its price path over the range you choose and lists the contracts resting at each price.
