Methodology

Exactly how each number on this site is produced, so you can reproduce it or refute it. Every formula here is either quoted from Kalshi's documentation or labelled as a choice this project made.

3. How a swing alert is raised

The collector stores one observation of each tracked market per cycle, stamped with the time the response was received. An alert is raised when the mid-price of the current observation differs from the immediately previous observation of the same market by at least $0.10 in absolute value:

qualifies = |mid_now − mid_previous| ≥ 0.10

Consequences, stated plainly. A move that happens and reverses between two polls is never seen. A large move spread over three polls may be split into three sub-threshold steps. A first observation of a market cannot produce an alert, because there is nothing to compare it with. These are limitations of poll-based detection, and they are flagged as F-11.

CaseBehaviourWhy
Prior observation has no mid (empty book)No alert, no change of 0An absent price is not a price
Market ticker changedNo comparisonComparing different contracts would be meaningless
Move of exactly $0.10AlertThe threshold is inclusive, as specified
Move of $0.099No alertBelow threshold

4. The research score (the model-filtered feed)

A single 0–100 number, used only to order the second feed. It is a triage heuristic written for this project. It is not return on investment, not a probability, and not Kalshi data.

ComponentWeightFormulaMissing input
Size of the move4040 × min(1, |Δ| / 0.40)scores 0 and is listed as missing
Crossable depth2525 × log(1+d) / log(1+5000)scores 0 and makes the row ineligible
Spread tightness1515 × max(0, 1 − spread / 0.20)scores 0
24h volume1010 × log(1+v) / log(1+500)scores 0
Freshness1010 × max(0, 1 − age / 900s)scores 0

A row is eligible for the filtered feed only if at least four of the five inputs exist and there is crossable depth above zero. That rule exists so a thin, unquotable market cannot score well by accident. The score has no backtest and no calibration; treat it as a reading order.
